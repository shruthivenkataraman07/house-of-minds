# Judge guide

Open https://house-of-minds.onrender.com. The free host can take about a minute to wake on the first visit. No account is needed.

**Navigation:** Overview · Machines · Operations · Council · Scenarios · Investment · Evidence · Validation · Answers.

- **Ask the House** (⌘K, or the header button) works from every view.
- **Glossary** (header) explains every term in plain English.
- Every "How this is calculated" panel shows the typeset formula, its symbols, the real numbers and their meaning.

## The four required interactions

1. **Change the analysis period.** Operations → Demand → change the period dates. The KPIs, calendar, weekly chart, zone matrix and "What changed" cards recalculate. Exploratory periods never change the six-month verdict or the July plan; those always use January–June.
2. **Drill into a bank or guest group.** Operations → Banks → select a bank to see its daily history and machines. Operations → Players → select a loyalty tier.
3. **Inspect model validation.** Validation shows the protocol windows, the model against the baseline, the error formulas with holdout values, and the event-day failures. Select any day on the chart.
4. **Trace a recommendation to evidence.** Investment → select the promotion segment to open its evidence passport: net = contribution − quantity × cost, the assumptions, council claims and source tables. Machines → select M-1158 → **Machine passport** shows the luck score, paying-spin test and gap breakdown with that machine's numbers.
