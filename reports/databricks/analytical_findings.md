## 1. The manager's bottom 10 mixes four different problems
Raw bottom 10 (`EV-C151E0401354`): M-5076, M-1158, M-1120, M-1118, M-1238, M-1181, M-1035, M-1179, M-1017, M-1265.

- **Location:** 9 of 10 sit in Back Forty, the lowest-demand zone ($11.71 theo per available hour vs the $18.15 floor median).
- **Exposure:** M-5076 (49 active days, luck-free demand rank #2 of 310); M-1120 (132 active days, luck-free demand rank #1 of 310); M-1118 (55 active days, luck-free demand rank #176 of 310). Short tenure explains the low total only where the per-hour rank is not also low.
- **Payout anomaly:** M-1158: demand ranks #46 of 310 on a luck-free basis; its win is low because it pays out too often (finding 2).
- **Game configuration:** 8 of the raw 10 run configurations whose same-zone peers earn under 80% of the zone rate (low house edge or small bets); 9 of 10 earn at least 90% of their same-config, same-zone peers.
- Shortfall decomposition summed over the raw 10 (`EV-E7D9F597BA06`): luck $-63,357, exposure $-133,046, location $-225,495, game_config $-135,324, machine_residual $3,545.
- Machines in both the raw and luck-aware bottom 10: M-1017, M-1035, M-1120, M-1179, M-1181, M-1238, M-1265, M-5076.

**Competing explanation tested: luck.** If the raw ranking were mostly luck, the Q1 bottom 10 would regress toward the middle in Q2. (`EV-955F2384D49D`): the Q1 raw bottom 10 averaged the 15% percentile of Q2 win per available hour; the luck-aware bottom 10 averaged 4%. Quarter-to-quarter rank correlation: theo/hour 0.987, win/hour 0.795. Low demand is persistent; luck is not the main driver, but the luck-free ranking is the more reliable one.

## 2. M-1158 pays out far more often than its game math allows (`EV-1C684B792E7B`)
Configured hit probability 31.5%, observed 38.6% over 674,454 spins (z = 124.2); |z| > 3 in 6 of 6 months. Observed hold 3.0% vs configured 10%; shortfall vs theo $36,165 ($200/day).
- Competing explanation (luck): rejected. Hit counts are not driven by jackpot size; the other 309 machines' hit z-scores have SD 1.006 (calibrated).
- Decision relevance: an inspection ($1,200) is the matching action; replacing the machine would hide the cause. Cause (paytable vs meter/software) unverified.

## 3. The walkway closure cut Back Forty demand by about 31% (`EV-D10316B9740D`)
Walkway W-ENT-B40 (Z-ENT<->Z-B40) closed for renovation, gaming dates 2025-03-03 to 2025-04-06. Relative to the rest of the floor (fixed machines, weekday adjusted), Back Forty theo per available hour changed -31.2% (95% CI -32.5% to -29.9%); after reopening -1.9% (p = 0.06). Estimated lost theo $162,185 (CI $152,669-$171,878).
- Competing explanation (floor-wide seasonality): controlled by the ratio design. Traffic counters agree: Back Forty's share of entries fell to 0.1727 of the rest (pre 0.2518).
- Decision relevance: half-year rankings understate Back Forty machines for a resolved, temporary cause. Back Forty is still the weakest zone before the closure, so access/visibility, not the machines, is the lever to test (e.g. wayfinding).
- Uncertainty: one event; the HAC interval assumes the rest of the floor is a valid control.

## 4. Ridgeline machines fail about 3.6x as often as Halcyon (`EV-65ECD7585FD8`)
Poisson rate ratio 3.635 (95% CI 3.124-4.229) after controlling for machine age (competing explanation: age; age matters but does not explain it). Floor downtime is only 0.38%; Ridgeline's excess costs about $5,072 theo per 30 days.
- Decision relevance: a $9,000 preventive sweep (7-day lead) would have to remove nearly all excess downtime to break even on theo within July; sensitivity needed in Step 2.

## 5. The comp program got cheaper for guests on 2025-05-01 (`EV-ABCA3B586A41`)
Points needed per comp dollar, pre -> post: Base 15.0 -> 7.0; Gold 12.0 -> 5.6; Platinum 9.0 -> 4.2.
- Competing explanation (tier mix): rejected; the rate halves within every tier.
- Comp cost per day $1,027 -> $1,105 (+7.6%); per carded theo 1.19% -> 1.31%.
- Decision relevance: answers Q5 ('did anything change'). Whether the cheaper comps bought extra play is untested; that is what A-COMP-PILOT would measure.

## Limitations

- Theo per available hour mixes location, game configuration and player mix.
- Lost-theo estimates assume downtime/closure hours would be played at average rates.
- No finding here is a funded recommendation; pricing and optimisation are Step 2+.
